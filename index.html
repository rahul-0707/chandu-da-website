The Sacred Aadi Kailash Yatra

The Aadi Kailash Yatra is a revered pilgrimage to a peak in the Himalayas of Uttarakhand, India, believed to be the second most important of the five Kailash peaks. The journey offers a unique blend of spiritual enlightenment and natural wonder, taking you through serene valleys, ancient villages, and majestic mountain passes.

This divine trek is a journey of a lifetime, where every step brings you closer to nature and the sacred, culminating in the darshan of the Aadi Kailash peak and the mystical Om Parvat.

Highlights of the Journey

Sacred Peaks & Lakes

Visit the divine Om Parvat and Aadi Kailash Parvat, and take a holy dip in the serene Parvati Sarovar and Gauri Kund.

Spiritual Destinations

Explore holy sites like Kali Mata Mandir, Jageshwar Dham, and Neemkaroli Kainchi Dham on your path to inner peace.

Natural Wonders

Discover the breathtaking beauty of places like Brahm Parvat, Panchchuli Parvat, and Shesh Nag Parvat.

The Sacred Aadi Kailash Yatra

The Aadi Kailash Yatra, also known as Chota Kailash, is a revered pilgrimage to a peak in the Himalayas of Uttarakhand, India, believed to be the second most important of the five Kailash peaks. The journey offers a unique blend of spiritual enlightenment and natural wonder.

This divine trek takes you through serene valleys, ancient villages, and majestic mountain passes, where every step brings you closer to nature and the sacred, culminating in the darshan of the Aadi Kailash peak and the mystical Om Parvat.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Common questions about the Adi Kailash and Om Parvat tours

What is the best time for Adi Kailash Yatra?

The best time for the Adi Kailash Yatra is during the summer months from May to June, and post-monsoon from September to October. During these periods, the weather is pleasant and the views of the Himalayan peaks are clear.

How to reach Om Parvat from Dharchula?

From Dharchula, the journey to Om Parvat involves traveling by road to Gunji or Nabhi via 4x4 vehicles, and then trekking or continuing by off-road vehicles towards the Om Parvat viewpoint (Nabhidang). Our tours make this completely seamless for you.
